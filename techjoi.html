The Technological Journal of Iraq

Bringing knowledge of the ever-evolving technology of the world to the Youth of Iraq

An offshoot of The Youth Science Journal Of Iraq - YSJOI

Our Mission

To continue on the work of YSJOI, and bringing with science a new frontier to the youth of Iraq - technology, ever evolving, and a field with deep dives aplenty.

We focus on creating articles with care. Rather than publishing a monthly issue as YSJOI does, we focus on creating periodic, sporadic articles of greater length, if less frequently.

By bridging the questions of YSJOI with deep dives into the technology of today, we aim to fill the gaps in knowledge available to the youth of Iraq in every way possible.

About the Journal

TechJOI is a platform engineered to track, learn and teach about the technological progress, constantly occuring everywhere, in Iraq.

With dives into technology including but not limites to AI infrastructure, coding, robotics, manufacturing and electronics, we aim to cover the ground between thought and action by actively teaching and learning about technology, to light the fire of curiosity in every youth of Iraq

Let's Talk About Sustainability and Climate Change.

Not limited to Iraq - this is an international problem. Weather changes, heatwaves, freak hurricanes. None of these follow the pattern of nature today; they are a result, directly, of the actions of humanity. Let's find out a bit more about how so many things - from our lifestyles, to enormous industries - affect the planet everyday. Below is an archive of articles, on sustainability, in a variety of areas; this is an opportunity for youth from everywhere to help spread awareness, and find out more in the process.
